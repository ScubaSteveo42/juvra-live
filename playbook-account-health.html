The Account Health Playbook

Your Account Health score is a lagging indicator of habits. The habits are the playbook.

WEEKLY: open the Account Health dashboard. Look at order defect rate, policy violations, shipping performance. Green is not "ignore it," green is "confirm it."

WITHIN 24 HOURS of any alert: read it, classify it, act. Every alert is one of five walls, and every wall has a tree in this checkpoint: unwanted storefront, insurance, verification, authenticity, IP.

THE STANDING RULES:

1. Dormant problems stay dormant. Never open a Support case to "ask about" a flag on a marketplace you don't use. Tree 1.

2. Exact match, always. Every document you ever submit matches your Seller Central legal name and address character for character, dated inside 180 days, printed to PDF from the source. Screenshots and edits read as forgeries.

3. Real invoices only. If you can't document it, you don't defend it. You take the listing down and keep the account. Forged paperwork is the only truly unrecoverable mistake on Amazon.

4. POA formula, every time one is asked for: root cause you own, corrective actions already taken, preventive steps going forward. Short, bulleted, zero emotion, specific dates and order numbers. Never blame Amazon, the brand, or the buyer.

5. Money has a clock. Reimbursement claims expire at ~60 days and pay out at your sourcing cost. Keep sourcing costs submitted so Amazon doesn't estimate low. Reimbursement Radar tracks the deadlines.

6. Reviews stay inside the lines. The "Request a Review" button, one ask per order, never incentivized, never filtered to happy customers.

Compliance isn't a fire drill, it's reps. Same as ungating: the sellers who win are the ones who respond fast, match exactly, and never get emotional with a reviewer.
