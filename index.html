amzOS powered by Juvra

amzOS is the operating system for your Amazon store — the student companion app for the Divine Amazon mentorship program.

Connect your inbox and amzOS automatically builds your order ledger, inventory sheet, and account-health alert radar from your Amazon seller emails (read-only Gmail access). It also includes the program's tools: ungating package builder, plan-of-action builder, reimbursement tracking, deal checking, and profit tracking.

amzOS uses Google's Gmail API with read-only access solely to detect Amazon seller emails (orders, shipments, account-health notices) and turn them into your ledger and alerts inside the app. Your email data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with third parties. amzOS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access is private to enrolled students of the Divine Amazon mentorship program. Loading the app requires JavaScript.
